Welcome to Manhattan

Gossip Girl is an American teen drama series set in New York City. The story follows a group of privileged teenagers living on the Upper East Side. Their lives are filled with fashion, romance, friendship, secrets and drama. An anonymous blogger known as Gossip Girl reveals their biggest secrets.
